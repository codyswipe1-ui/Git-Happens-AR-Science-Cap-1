About the Project

ImmerseED is an interactive science education application designed to help elementary and middle school students better understand cell structures and their functions through 3D visualization and augmented reality (AR).

Our goal is to make learning science more engaging, accessible, and interactive by allowing students to explore biological cells beyond traditional 2D diagrams.




The following features are planned for ImmerseED:

Interactive 3D Models: Rotate, zoom, and explore detailed cell models.

Exploded View: Separate and hide different parts of a cell to better understand its structure.

Guided Lessons: Learn about cell organelles and their functions.

Interactive Quizzes: Test knowledge through educational assessments.

Cell Type Selection: Explore different cell types, including plant and animal cells.

Augmented Reality: View and interact with 3D cell models in real-world environments using compatible devices.

Progress Tracking: Track completed lessons and quizzes.




ImmerseED is designed for:

Students in grades 4–8

Educators teaching introductory cell biology

Learners interested in interactive science education
